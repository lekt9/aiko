# Aiko as an AI Forward Deployed Engineer

**Status classes:** **Proven** means a runnable gate exists; **Designed** means
specified but not witnessed; **Absent** means not in this tree. Public copy must
not promote a capability beyond this boundary.

## Product boundary

Aiko targets recurring, cross-system operational loops where an incorrect claim
has an external cost. It is hired into one tenant-scoped role, with least
privilege and an outcome, rather than installed as a bag of integrations.
“The first AI FDE” remains **Absent**: there is no competitive witness.

This document is not a product-oneshot. A seat roster, dispatch board,
Company OS sheet, or any other invented operator spreadsheet is not the
FDE surface. Those defaults come from an unrelated product pack; they do not
apply here. Self-wiring means digest-admitted harnesses (Cell organelles,
Neuron limbs, delegated seats via `deploy-aiko`), not a CRUD grid of roles.

DeepSeek Harness is cited, not fused. Official DSH, Creator Mode,
`dsh-agent-teams`, and `dsh-continual-evolve` are reference checkouts only.
See [`DSH-HARNESS-MAP.md`](DSH-HARNESS-MAP.md). They are not a published
Aiko layer and must not appear as a live Worker claiming to be the FDE.

## W6H role contract

| Question | Binding answer |
|---|---|
| **Who** | One tenant-scoped role acting through verified, delegated authority; never caller-supplied lineage or a borrowed personal session. |
| **What** | A digest-pinned typed harness, admitted limbs, cited Contract context, and independently settled effects. |
| **When** | On an event, cadence, reply, invalidation, or explicit approval; uncertain work remains pending. |
| **Where** | Smithers hosts; Cell/runCell types the harness; Contract owns durable authority and verdicts; Neuron executes admitted limbs. |
| **Why** | Leave an inspectable, handoff-safe operational loop whose claims do not rely on Aiko's report. |
| **How** | Admit verified ingress → retrieve cited context → execute least authority → read back → settle, retry, or compensate. |

## Native ownership

The old Python FDE prototypes are removed. Their authoritative replacements are:

- **Contract:** durable context projections, lifecycle, delegated capabilities,
  execution recovery, retention, and graph verdicts.
- **Neuron:** digest admission, bounded authoring, and the admitted Unbrowse and
  provider-effect limbs.
- **Smithers:** Contract-backed lifecycle/control composition.
- **Cell/runCell:** the preserved typed harness. It is not a credential store,
  context plane, readiness ledger, or durable control authority.

There is no root workflow-binding/control store and no duplicate Python
credential, context, authoring, Unbrowse, or Telethon authority under Cell.
`tests/no-duplicate-fde-primitives-gate.sh` is the negative witness and also
runs the Smithers Cell/runCell tests so deletion of the harness cannot satisfy it.

## Authority and handoff

- Public ingress authenticates before emitting typed verified authority.
- Credentials cross the Rust input membrane as opaque handles; secret material
  is not copied into prompts, Contract evidence, Cell state, or Smithers state.
- Context is purpose-scoped and cited from Contract; transcripts and handoff
  blobs do not become authority.
- Generated bytes are inert until digest admission and real-path judgment.
- Effects settle only from independent read-back. A crossed boundary is not
  blindly crossed again; invalidation opens recomputation or compensation.
- The admitted Neuron package and typed Cell harness remain executable without
  turning the Aiko host into a second ledger.
