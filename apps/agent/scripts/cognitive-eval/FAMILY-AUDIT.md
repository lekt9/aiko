## Consequences

- Report raw case numerators and denominators, never 60 independently held-out families or population-level confidence intervals. Validation here is a reserved local mechanism check, not blind evaluation.
- `current-control` is current source with a mechanism disabled, not the preserved pre-change implementation. The proactive `direct-notify-silent` arm uses the existing legacy reflection contract, not a newly tuned model policy. Both use supplied offline decisions; protocol differences and deterministic checks cannot measure human usefulness or autonomous policy selection.
- Work proactive drivers use explicit synthetic initial work/receipt state and real native coordinator transitions. They do not establish upstream receipt collection, source truth or real correspondence authority. Existing secretary regressions remain separate evidence.
- The fixed protected gate still requires a sealed exact current source/dataset and complete passing five-case validation report. Passing it would permit only a reserved mechanism probe, not justify an unseen-task claim. This audit does not authorize execution, candidate tuning, paid inference or rollout.
- For a held-out performance claim, an independent author must create fresh family-level splits after the candidate is frozen. Variants sharing input, outcome contract or causal mechanism must stay together. Log authoring provenance and exclusions before execution; never tune to protected outcomes.

Automatic checks enforce 60 unique case IDs and 10/5/5 per category, not independence. Full phase 0 acceptance remains open until independent provenance, equivalent pre-change/simple/individual/combined baselines and the fixed scoring contract are supplied.
